# nonprismatic-control.stl — A9 negative control

**Purpose.** A deliberately non-prismatic fixture for RULE 5.1. It must decline at
condition **3** (oblique plane) or **5** (cylinder extent off a cap level), and
must **not** be constructed so that the only failure is condition 1.

**Geometry.** A 12-sided hexagonal tube (outer R=10 mm, inner hole R=4 mm)
standing on +Z. The outer and inner walls are two recognized cylinders that
share an axis, so the predicate passes conditions 1–2. The bottom is a cap
plane; the top is a single sloped plane (`z = 8 + 0.25 x`) whose normal is
neither a cap nor a lateral — an **oblique**. Intended decline: condition 3.

**Provenance.** Authored 2026-08-30 for AC3-P1 A9. Synthetic, not a CAD export.
Generated by `tests/unit/gen_nonprismatic.py` (stdlib only). Not special-cased
in `src/`; the predicate sees it as any other `RegionSet`.
