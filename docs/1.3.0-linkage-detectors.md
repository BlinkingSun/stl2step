## Cone math (lane 130-CONE-MATH, SPEC-130-cone-math)

Published signatures — `src/refit_math.hpp`, implemented in `src/refit_math.cpp`,
certified by `cone_math_unit` (`tests/unit/cone_math_test.cpp`). Pure `gp_`
geometry: no `MeshView`, no `Region`, no build-path change. `refit_internal.hpp`
does not move; the second header exists so the unit test can compile the math TU
on its own.

Frame, OCCT `Geom_ConicalSurface` / `gp_Cone` (the untrimmed surface is the
complete **double** cone, infinite in `v` both ways):

```
S(u,v) = O + (R + v*sin(Ang)) * (cos(u)*XDir + sin(u)*YDir) + v*cos(Ang)*ZDir
z = v*cos(Ang)          rho_cone(z) = R + z*tan(Ang)   (SIGNED; < 0 past the apex)
z_apex = -R/tan(Ang)    v_apex      = -R/sin(Ang)      v = z/cos(Ang)
```

`Ang = SemiAngle()` is non-zero in `]-PI/2, PI/2[` and may be negative (apex on
the positive side of `ZDir`); every formula below is stated for signed `Ang`.

**Classes** (`enum class ConeDevClass`, `coneDevClassName`, `coneDevClassIsExact`):

| class | when | value |
|---|---|---|
| `CoaxialRim` — `"circle-on-cone"` | circle plane ⟂ axis, centre **on** axis | **exact**, constant in `u` |
| `PerpPlane` — `"circle-on-cone-perp"` | circle plane ⟂ axis, centre **off** axis | **exact** supremum |
| `GeneralBound` — `"circle-on-cone-bound"` | tilted and/or off-axis | **provable upper bound** |
| `Unhandled` — `"unhandled-circle-on-cone"` | degenerate input | `-1.0` |

`"circle-on-cone"` is the class D-130-3 gates cone faces on. A `GeneralBound` is
never presented as the deviation: it is `>=` the true supremum, so a bind site may
compare it against a tolerance, and a comparison that fails means tier 2 (mesh
polyline, counted per D-130-2), not a widened tolerance.

**Exact point-to-surface distance.** The meridian plane through `p` meets the
double cone in two full lines, both entirely on the surface, so the perpendicular
foot is always a real surface point — no apex clamping, no ray case:

```
pointConeDist(cone, p) = cos(Ang) * | rho_p - |rho_cone(z_p)| |
```

**The supremum.** With `xi = |rho_cone(z)|` and the circle's radial span
`[rho_lo, rho_hi]`, `sup = cos(Ang) * max(rho_hi - xi, xi - rho_lo)`. For a
circle in a plane ⟂ the axis the span is exactly `[|d0 - r|, d0 + r]` (`d0` =
centre-to-axis distance), which is why (a) is exact and collapses on the axis to
`cos(Ang) * | r - |rho_cone(z)| |`. For (b) the same exact formula is evaluated
over an interval box — `z` in `z0 +- r*sin(beta)` (tight; `beta` = angle between
circle normal and cone axis) and `rho` in
`[max(0, r*cos(beta) - d0, d0 - r), d0 + r]` (the circle projects onto the plane
⟂ the axis as an ellipse of semi-axes `r` and `r*cos(beta)`, so the projected
offset has modulus in `[r*cos(beta), r]` and the triangle inequality gives both
ends) — whose maximum is taken at a corner. One evaluator serves both regimes:
at `sin(beta) = 0`, `cos(beta) = 1` the box collapses onto the exact span and the
inequality becomes an equality. The bound therefore stays tight where it matters
— a rim tilted 0.05° off a 45° cone of radius 11 bounds at 6.7907e-3 mm against a
true supremum of 6.7907e-3 mm, not O(r).

**The class is invariant under rigid placement.** A circle whose normal *is* the
cone's axis direction classes `CoaxialRim`/`PerpPlane` for every orientation of
that cone in world space. That is not automatic: two unit `gp_Dir`s that are
bitwise (anti)parallel still dot to `1 -+ 1ulp`, and `sqrt(1 - dot*dot)` turns
that ulp into a spurious tilt of ~1e-8 rad — eight orders above
`Precision::Angular()`. `sin(beta)` therefore comes from the **cross product**,
which is exactly zero for identical coordinates and carries absolute rather than
square-rooted error near parallel. Measured on 2000 random placements of the B1
cone, the `sqrt` form demoted 669 exact rims to `GeneralBound` and reported
deviations up to 1.55e-7 mm — above `Precision::Confusion()`, so it would not
have been absorbed at the bind site either. `cone_math_unit` sweeps 400
placements and asserts 0 demotions; it is red against the `sqrt` form.

The five scalar helpers (`coneRadiusAt`, `coneApexZ`, `coneApexV`, `coneVAtZ`,
`coneVAtRadius`) return **NaN**, never a plausible 0 or inf, for a cone that
could not have come from a legal `gp_Cone`.

**Apex exclusion.** `coneFrustumVRange` refuses unless the signed cone radius is
strictly positive at both rims. That single condition *is* apex exclusion:
`rho_cone(z) = tan(Ang) * (z - z_apex)`, so two positive radii put both rims on
the same side of the apex and `[vLo, vHi]` cannot contain `v_apex`. CONE-BUILD
trims to that range and never has to special-case the apex.

**Tier-1 refusals.** `coneIntCylCircle` returns only the near-nappe root
`z = (R_cyl - R)/tan(Ang)`; the far-nappe root `z = (-R_cyl - R)/tan(Ang)` lies
below the apex, hence outside any frustum's `v`-range. `coneIntConeCircle`
solves the branch `rho_a(z) == rho_b(z)` with both **signed** radii positive; it
refuses parallel generators, and refuses the two other branches (both signed
radii equal and negative — a real circle, but on the far nappe of both cones —
and `rho_a == -rho_b`, one nappe each), neither of which can lie in the v-range
of a frustum. Non-coaxial and oblique configurations return `false` for all
three — tier 2 by D-130-2, never an approximated conic.

All three agree with `IntAna_QuadQuadGeo` on the battery numbers — the same OCCT
code `pickIntAna` runs at the bind site — and `pointConeDist` agrees with
`GeomAPI_ProjectPointOnSurf` to 1.7e-14 mm over 1105 probes, including points far
past the apex. `cone_math_unit`: **274/274 PASS in 0.19 s**.

**What CONE-BUILD still has to add.** `circleOnConeMax` is the surface-distance
supremum only, by design — it takes `gp_` types and no `Geom_`/pcurve handles, so
it stays pure. `circleOnCylMax` (`src/refit_build.cpp:6814`) additionally maxes in
`curveSurfDevAtBind` at the two bind parameters; the cone branch of
`exactMaxAtBind` must do the same, i.e.
`max(curveSurfDevAtBind(c3, f, ...), curveSurfDevAtBind(c3, l, ...), circleOnConeMax(...))`,
and map the class with `coneDevClassName`. Omitting the pcurve term would record
a tolerance that ignores the parametrisation, which is a different supremum from
the one this lane certifies.
